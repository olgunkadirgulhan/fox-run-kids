Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The Fox Run Kids publishing tool is used only by the channel owner to upload original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • The tool requests access to the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos, set thumbnails and read the channel ID.
  • OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never shared or sold.
  • The tool does not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or of any other users, including children.
  • Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
